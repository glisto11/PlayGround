## NEXUS DUEL 24

A self-contained neon card duel for the browser.

- **Auto room:** on startup the game creates your private room, auto-joins it, and shows the live online room list.
- **24-zone field:** 6 unit zones and 6 tech zones per duelist. The full arena is 24 zones.
- **8000 → 0 LP:** summon units, cast tech cards, attack directly, and drive the opponent's life points to zero.
- **Always moving:** cards float, deploy, lunge, take hits, and shatter. Every action is animated.

### Play

Open the preview. The boot sequence creates and joins your room, then the duel starts automatically.

- Tap a hand card, then tap a highlighted zone.
- Tap one of your units, then tap an enemy unit to attack. When the enemy front row is empty, tap KAI or an empty enemy zone for a direct attack.
- Each turn gives 3 AP. Summoning, casting, and attacking each cost 1 AP.
- Clear the enemy front row and keep attacking until their LP reach 0.
